Detailing Packages & Pricing

Pick the level of shine your vehicle needs — we bring everything to you.

Essential Wash

$80

A full exterior hand wash, wheel cleaning, and tire shine, plus a vacuum of the mats and floor area and a wipe-down of the steering wheel, shifter, and screen. This is the quick, affordable option for keeping your car looking clean day to day — perfect if you just need the dirt and grime gone without a deep clean underneath.

Most Popular

Signature Detail

$180

A complete inside-and-out clean: exterior wash, wheel cleaning, spray-on wax, tire shine, and streak-free glass outside; a full vacuum and hand-wipe of every surface inside — console, cup holders, screens, seats, dash, door jambs, panels, pedals, and mats — with trim dressing wherever it's needed. This is the package for anyone who wants their car to actually feel new again, not just look wiped down.

Book This Package

Premium Detail

$260

Our most thorough service, built for cars that need real restoration. On top of a full exterior wash and complete interior clean, we spot-treat visible stains in the carpets, seats, and mats, clean built-up dirt off the doors, bugs from the bumper, dress the trim inside and out, and finish with a clay bar treatment for paint that's smooth to the touch, not just clean. This is the level of care for anyone who wants their car looking and feeling its absolute best, not just clean.
